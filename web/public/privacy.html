Duy My Linear privacy policy

Duy My Linear is a private issue tracker run by {{operator}} for a small invited team. Only people an administrator has invited by email can sign in.

What Duy My Linear stores

  • From Google sign-in: your name, email address and profile photo. Duy My Linear asks Google for nothing else, and never sees your Google password.
  • What you write in it: issues, comments, reactions, and a record of who changed what and when.
  • From GitHub: the title, number, branch, author and state of pull requests in the team's GitHub organization that mention an issue.
  • A session cookie that keeps you signed in. Duy My Linear uses no tracking or advertising cookies.

Who can see it

Only members of the team who are signed in to Duy My Linear, and tools a member connects with their own personal token (for example Claude). Duy My Linear does not sell, rent or share your data with anyone, and does not use it for advertising.

Where it lives

On a server operated by {{operator}}.

Removing your data

Ask an administrator to remove your account, or email the address below to have your account and personal information deleted.
